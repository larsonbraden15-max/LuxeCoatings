About Luxe Coatings

At Luxe Coatings LLC, we believe your space should look as good as it performs. We specialize in high-quality coating solutions designed to give floors and surfaces a durable, clean, and professional finish.

From garages and workshops to residential and commercial spaces, we take the time to properly prepare each surface and deliver a finish built to last.

We work closely with every customer to understand their needs, explain their options, and create a finished product that matches their vision.
